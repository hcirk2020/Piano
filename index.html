HOW TO PLAY
In Play in Time, hit the note when its head reaches the gold line. In At Your Own Pace, the score waits for the correct key and advances immediately. The active library contains score-referenced melody studies, not complete piano transcriptions. Pitch spellings and note order are checked against the linked score references. Each song’s audit note identifies octave folding, omitted voices, and timing simplifications. Some excerpts preserve source note values; others use evenly spaced practice durations because the text transcription does not expose every rhythm. Generated backing is a practice aid, not the original accompaniment.
